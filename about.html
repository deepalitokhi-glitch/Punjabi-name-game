About Punjabi Name Place Animal Thing

Sat Sri Akal Ji! This is a fun and educational word game made with love in Punjab.

Punjabi Name Place Animal Thing Game helps kids and adults improve their vocabulary, memory and thinking speed. The game is simple - you get a letter and you have to think of a Name, Place, Animal and Thing starting with that letter.

This game is inspired by our childhood notebook game which we used to play in school. Now we have made it digital so you can play anytime.

Created by: Deepali Tokhi - A game lover from Punjab who wants to make learning fun.

Our Mission: To make simple, fun and educational games for everyone without any heavy ads or payments. Just pure fun!

Enjoy the game and share with your friends!
