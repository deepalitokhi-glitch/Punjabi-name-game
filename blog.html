How to Play Punjabi Name Place Animal Thing Game? Full Guide

Published: Nov 2026 | By Deepali Tokhi

Do you remember that school game where we used to write Name, Place, Animal, Thing in our notebooks? We have brought the same fun online!

What is Name Place Animal Thing Game?

It is a word puzzle game where you get a random letter (like A, B, C). You have to think of 4 words starting with that letter:

  • Name: A person's name - e.g., Arshdeep for A
  • Place: A city or country - e.g., Amritsar for A
  • Animal: An animal name - e.g., Anaconda for A
  • Thing: An object - e.g., Apple for A

How to Score Points?

For every correct set, you get 10 points. The game will automatically take you to the next letter from A to Z. Try to complete all 26 letters! It is a great way to improve your vocabulary and brain speed.

Benefits of This Game

1. Improves English vocabulary for kids and adults
2. Increases thinking speed and memory
3. Fun for family and friends - you can challenge each other
4. No download needed - play directly on mobile or laptop
5. Completely free and safe for kids

Tips to Win

Always keep a few names ready for tough letters like Q, X, Z. For example: For X - Xavier (Name), Xalapa (Place), Xerus (Animal), Xylophone (Thing). Practice daily and your speed will increase!
